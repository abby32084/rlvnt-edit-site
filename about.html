The publication

The RLVNT Edit is an editorial standard for high-end home and lifestyle objects worth keeping — premium outdoor furniture, mattresses, home gym, premium kitchen, considered home decor. We curate exclusively in the high-end purchase tier and we recommend pieces that earn their place through material honesty and construction worth the price across the long arc of ownership.

We accept no payment for inclusion. No brand reviews our editorial work before publication. When we include affiliate links, commission flows to The RLVNT Edit at no additional cost to the reader, and that arrangement never determines what we cover or how. Our full editorial disclosure lays out the standard in detail.

The editor

Abby Buchmiller is the founder of 1982 Media LLC and the editor of The RLVNT Edit. She previously co-founded a $250M+ solar services company that scaled to 500+ employees over four years. She brings two decades of operating instinct to the editorial standard at The RLVNT Edit — ethical channel growth, compliance hygiene, and a partnership-grade approach to the brands we cover.
