The Editorial Standard

We earn our recommendation, or we don't make one.

Every product in every guide is evaluated against the same frame: materials honesty, construction worth the price, and a useful life measured in years rather than seasons.

We accept no payment for inclusion. No brand reviews these guides before publication. When we include affiliate links, commission flows to The RLVNT Edit at no additional cost to the reader — and that arrangement never determines what we cover or how.
