Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated · May 2026

The RLVNT Edit is reader-supported editorial. Some of the products we cover include affiliate links, which means we may earn a small commission if you click through and make a qualifying purchase — at no additional cost to you. This page explains exactly how that works and what it does (and does not) influence.

What is an affiliate link?

An affiliate link is a URL that includes a unique tracking identifier tying a purchase back to the publication that referred you. When you click an affiliate link on The RLVNT Edit and make a qualifying purchase, the retailer pays us a small percentage of the sale. The price you pay is exactly the same as it would be if you arrived at the retailer directly.

Which programs we participate in

The RLVNT Edit is a participant in affiliate programs including, but not limited to:

  • Amazon Associates & the Amazon Influencer Program
  • Partnerize (including Saatva and Glacier)
  • Impact
  • CJ Affiliate (Commission Junction)
  • AWIN
  • Direct merchant affiliate programs as they apply

As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases.

How affiliate revenue influences our coverage

It doesn't. Our editorial standard is independent of which products pay the most commission. We curate the products we believe are worth the space they take up — in your home, your routine, your budget.

Specifically:

  • We never accept payment to write about a product.
  • We never write about a product we wouldn't recommend if there were no commission attached.
  • We do not run sponsored posts, paid placements, or brand-partnership ad units in editorial.
  • Commission rates do not influence ranking, framing, or whether a product is covered at all.

How we disclose

Every piece of content that contains affiliate links carries a clear disclosure — on the page itself, on accompanying social posts, and in the footer of this site. We follow the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's Endorsement Guides and intend our disclosures to be conspicuous, unambiguous, and consistent.
