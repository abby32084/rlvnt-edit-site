The Best Premium Outdoor Furniture
A Considered Buyer's Guide for 2026

By Abby Buchmiller · Editor, The RLVNT Edit · May 2026

Outdoor furniture occupies a strange place in the home goods market. Most of what's sold is engineered to look acceptable for two seasons and disintegrate by the third.

Particleboard frames wrapped in synthetic wicker, aluminum joints that pit by year two, cushion fills whose foam compresses into uselessness after a single summer of UV exposure. The pieces in this guide are the opposite proposition. Teak that silvers gracefully over twenty years. Powder-coated steel rated for coastal environments. Cushion fills wrapped in solution-dyed acrylic that holds color through a decade of direct sun.

The RLVNT Edit's standard for outdoor is straightforward: we recommend pieces that earn their place through material honesty and construction that justifies the price across the long arc of ownership. Every item below has been evaluated against that frame.

01

The Teak Dining Set That Outlasts the House

Brand
Restoration Hardware
Price
Starting from $4,000; full sets $8,000–$12,000
Materials
Solid Grade A Burmese teak, stainless steel hardware

Solid Grade A Burmese teak with mortise-and-tenon joinery — not the dowel-and-glue construction common at lower price points. Teak's natural silica content means the wood weathers to a silver patina without protective coating, and the dense grain resists rot, insects, and dimensional warping across decades of outdoor exposure. Stainless steel hardware throughout. The set sets a patio for the next twenty years rather than the next two summers.
