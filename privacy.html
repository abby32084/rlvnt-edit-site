Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 2026

The RLVNT Edit ("we," "us," "the publication") is published by 1982 Media LLC. This privacy policy describes how we handle information collected through this website.

Information we collect

We collect minimal information. Specifically:

  • Aggregate analytics — non-identifying data about pages visited, country-level location, and referring source. We use this to understand which editorial content resonates.
  • Email address — only if you voluntarily provide it (e.g., for editorial newsletter signup). We never sell, rent, or share these addresses.
  • Affiliate-link click data — when you click an affiliate link, the destination retailer's tracking system records the referral. We do not personally identify clickers.

Cookies

This site uses minimal cookies — strictly functional and aggregate analytics. Affiliate retailers may set their own cookies when you click through to their sites; their cookie policies govern that behavior.

Third-party services

We use third-party services to operate the publication, including affiliate networks (Amazon Associates, Partnerize, Impact, CJ Affiliate, AWIN), web hosting (Replit / Vercel), and standard web analytics. Each maintains its own privacy policy governing how it handles data.

Your rights

If you live in a jurisdiction with applicable privacy regulations (GDPR, CCPA, etc.), you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us. To make any such request, email abby@rlvnt.media.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after a change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
